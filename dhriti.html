ARE YOU STILL SAD????

Do you want to see something that’ll make you happy? 🥺

for you, Dhriti 💙

I wish I could make everything feel a little lighter for you right now. I know some days get too loud, thoughts get too heavy, and even the smallest things can feel like a lot.

But please remember this: you do not have to be okay every second. You don’t have to explain every feeling, find the perfect words, or pretend to smile just to make everyone else comfortable.

You are allowed to pause.
You are allowed to cry.
You are allowed to have a difficult day and still be loved through every bit of it.

Dhriti, I hope you speak to yourself with the same kindness you so easily deserve from others. Drink a little water, take a slow breath, rest if you need to.

💙

Breathe in as the heart grows… and out as it shrinks. 🌙
One hard moment is not your whole story. This feeling will move, little by little, and you don’t have to rush it.

Bad days are just clouds. They pass, and the sky behind them is still yours, full of stars. ✨

And listen…
whenever you’re sad, even I become sad 😓
because you matter so much to me.

I never want you to feel that you have to hide your sadness for my sake. You don’t owe me a happy version of yourself.

I just want you to feel safe enough to be exactly as you are. If I could, I’d sit beside you quietly, hold your hand, and remind you that you’re not alone.

Thank you for being you. The world is softer and brighter with you in it, and my life is better because you became a part of it.

I hope something makes you smile today—even if it’s just this little page. 💙
— Siddh
